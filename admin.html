How this version works

Choose the product photo directly from your computer. You do not need to type an image location or upload the photo separately. The selected image is embedded into the generated products.js file.
  1. Enter the product name, category and description.
  2. Click Choose File and select the real product photo from your computer.
  3. Click Add Product. Repeat for your other products.
  4. Click Download products.js.
  5. Upload the downloaded products.js to the root of your GitHub repository and replace the old one.
  6. Wait a little for GitHub Pages to publish the update.

Tip: Use reasonably sized JPG/PNG photos (around 1–2 MB or less each) so the generated file stays manageable.
